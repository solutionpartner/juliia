Maison de Beauté

Beauty kept on record, not made to burn out.

Every JULIIA formula is written — colour, scent and care logged against the skin it was built for. Nothing seasonal, nothing disposable. An archive you return to, the way you return to a voice.

The House

One house.
Three crafts.

JULIIA began as a single counter — a table, a lamp, and three tested formulas with no claim attached to them but one: that each held a true colour, a true scent, a true effect. We have not left that table behind so much as carried it with us, into pigment mills and perfume ateliers and cold rooms where concentrates are kept like archives.

Makeup, fragrance and skincare share the same studio and the same rulebook: small batches, finite raw materials, and a refusal to reformulate for a season. When something is done, we retire it — we do not dilute it.

“Nothing we make is discarded to be fashionable. It is either kept on the archive, or it leaves the house entirely.”

— The studio, Paris

Notes & Craft

Written down,
kept slow.

Each JULIIA piece is logged with its raw materials, its batch, and the skin or air it was measured against. These are the notes of the archive — the pigments held true, the eaux drawn, the concentrates kept in the cold room. Read them like a record of the house itself.

  • PIG.

    Eight pigments, held true

    Milled in small batches, matched to near-black and lit red. Each is logged against a skin tone so its colour survives, not the season.

    08
  • EAU.

    Three eaux, drawn slowly

    Extracts left to settle on wool cloth. Only the top note survives to the bottle; the rest is released back to the air.

    03
  • SOIN.

    Four concentrates, kept cold

    Stored at two degrees to hold their measure. Applied sparingly — a concentrate earns its place by effect, not by volume.

    04
  • ARCH.

    One archive, never reset

    Every formula that leaves the house is kept on the record. Nothing is discarded to be fashionable.

    01
